# EFF Short Wordlist #1 attribution

This directory vendors the words from the Electronic Frontier Foundation's
[Short Wordlist #1](https://www.eff.org/files/2016/09/08/eff_short_wordlist_1.txt).
EFF describes the source list as 1,296 memorable, distinct words with at most
five characters.

The material is used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

For DNS-label safety, Intar excludes the source entry `yo-yo` while retaining
the separate entry `yoyo`. Route generation therefore samples uniformly from
1,295 entries and always produces exactly four hyphen-delimited word tokens.
