The AI comparison is optional. The required two-fault path runs fully offline. Only a
learner who independently has a trusted assistant available should generate the four-hour
read-only kubeconfig; never spend workshop time installing a client, creating an account,
or fixing provider connectivity.

The useful prompt shape asks for one root-cause hypothesis, the exact observations that
would prove it, and confidence. The human then runs those observations against the live
cluster. Fault 4 makes the obvious diagnosis plausible and wrong, so the verification
habit lands whether the first hypothesis came from a model or a partner.

No agent is the expected offline case: one learner plays "confident AI" from manifests,
the other falsifies the claim against the cluster. Same muscle, no dependency.
