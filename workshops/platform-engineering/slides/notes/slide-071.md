The ladder is deliberate, and the first two rungs are the complete offline path.

Step 1, Signal: the Cloudbox Console application detail gives the unhealthy condition—
CrashLoopBackOff, ProgressDeadlineExceeded, ImagePullBackOff—without guessing.

Step 2, Hint: a deterministic cause-to-action hint narrows the next observation. Most
incidents should resolve here; that is the design working.

Step 3, Optional agent: a learner who independently has network access and a provider
credential may compare a read-only model hypothesis with their own. It is not part of
the timer, verifier, hints, solution, helper flow, or checkpoint recovery.

The pedagogical point is that an agent is the last resort, not the first reflex. Whatever
produces the hypothesis, the learner must kill or confirm it against live evidence.
