The Intar room owns the help contract. **Need help** creates a persistent queue item that a
helper can claim. Browser-terminal access still needs a learner-granted, time-limited
assist grant; native SSH and raw terminal history remain learner-only.

Pairing is encouraged for reasoning, but every learner keeps their own persistent
workspace and progress. A broken guest is recovered from a canonical checkpoint or
reprovisioned on an eligible organization runner—never by asking the learner to install
tools, switch to a personal laptop, or open a cloud-development lifeboat.
