The task is intentionally small: open the provisioned terminal, change to
`/opt/platform-engineering-workshop/lab/00-setup`, and run `./verify.sh`.

The control room should show presence, provisioning, technical verification, and current
probe health as separate states. Do not advance because a learner says "it looks fine."
Conversely, a late learner restored from a predecessor checkpoint is caught up, not
retroactively verified for skipped technical work.

Helpers work the **Need help** queue. Missing tools, images, resources, or guest kernel
capabilities are never learner setup tasks.
