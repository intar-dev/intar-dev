The sticker everyone's seen — say it with the shrug it deserves, then take the other half seriously.

The joke is true, and that's the good news. "The cloud" is not exotic hardware; it's ordinary computers plus software that turns them into rentable primitives. The hyperscaler's moat was never the metal — the organization runner underneath each Intar workspace is ordinary infrastructure. The moat is the software on top: the APIs, the automation, the self-service. And that software — all of it — is open source.

So the meme is our thesis, inverted: if a cloud is just someone else's computer, then your computer, plus the right open-source control plane, is a cloud. That's the whole workshop in one line.
