# Hint 1: The workspace is still provisioning

Stay in the Intar workshop room and watch the workspace status. Provisioning starts only
after check-in and the facilitator's bulk-provision action. Do not open a second sandbox:
Intar permits one active runtime slot per learner.

If provisioning reports an allocation or image-cache failure, click **Need help**. That
is a runner-capacity issue for the facilitator, not something to fix inside a terminal.
